The Repricing

Everyone has the same tools now. That's the problem.

Campaign builds that took a team a month now take one operator an afternoon. Blog posts that cost hundreds now cost pennies. The production layer of marketing has been repriced to near zero — and every agency, every in-house team, every competitor has access to the same collapse.

Cheap production doesn't create an advantage. It removes one. When everyone can generate the same volume of content, at the same speed, with the same tools, the thing that separates outcomes stops being output — and starts being the judgment behind it: what to make, who it's for, and what never should have shipped.
